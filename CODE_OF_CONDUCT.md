# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming, patient and respectful, especially to people who are new to operating systems or security.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and criticising code and ideas rather than people.
- Assuming good faith, and asking a clarifying question before assuming a mistake.
- Crediting others' work and being honest about what a change does and does not prove &mdash; this project values accuracy over impressiveness.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces &mdash; issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews &mdash; and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate them, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@tejgokani](https://github.com/tejgokani),
using the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
promptly, fairly and confidentially. (Security vulnerabilities go through
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not here.)
